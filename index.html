TTSH — TikTok Side Hustle

Legal documents for the TTSH desktop application.
Local-first desktop app

TTSH is a desktop application that a creator runs on their own Windows computer to help produce short videos and upload them to their own TikTok account's drafts. It processes content locally and connects to TikTok only to upload the user's own videos for the user to review and post.
