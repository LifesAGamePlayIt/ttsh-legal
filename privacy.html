Privacy Policy

TTSH — TikTok Side Hustle (desktop application)
Effective 2026-11-09

This Privacy Policy explains how the TTSH desktop application ("TTSH", "the app", "we") handles information. TTSH is a tool that runs entirely on the user's own computer. We do not operate a server that collects your data, and we do not sell, rent, or share your information.

1. Who we are

TTSH is provided by Adam Weaver ("the developer"). For any question about this policy, contact adamweaver123456@gmail.com.

2. What the app accesses

  • Your TikTok account (with your permission). When you link an account, TikTok's OAuth flow grants the app the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes. The app uses these only to show your account name and to upload videos you create to your own TikTok inbox as drafts for you to review and post.
  • Media you provide. Video files, audio, and text you supply or generate are processed locally on your computer to assemble videos.

3. Where your data is stored

All application data — including TikTok access tokens, account settings, your job queue, logs, and finished videos — is stored locally on your own computer (under your user data folder). Access tokens never leave your machine except to authenticate directly with TikTok's official API. We, the developer, have no access to them.

4. Information sent off your computer

TTSH is local-first. It makes outbound network connections only for:

  • TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com) — to authenticate your account and upload your videos as drafts.
  • AI model downloads — to download open-source language and speech models to run locally (e.g. via Ollama).
  • Trend lookups (optional) — public, read-only requests to sources such as Reddit, Google Trends, and TikTok's public Creative Center to surface trending topics. These requests do not include your personal data.
  • First-run setup downloads — program components needed to run.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.

5. TikTok data use & deletion

Data obtained through the TikTok API is used solely to provide the app's upload feature to you, consistent with TikTok's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines. Because tokens and data are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time by removing the account in the app (which deletes its stored tokens) or by uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder. You may also revoke the app's access from your TikTok account settings.

6. Children

TTSH is not directed to children and is intended for use by adult content creators managing their own accounts.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.

8. Contact
